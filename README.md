# Retail Sales Visualization & Business Insights

## Introduction

This analysis uses the SuperStore dataset to understand sales, profit, discounts, and numerical relationships using Seaborn and Matplotlib.

## Findings

Bar plots compare sales and profit across categories, while box plots show their distribution and outliers.

## Discount Analysis

The discount-profit relationship shows that higher discounts can reduce profit margins. Excessive discounts may lead to low or negative profits.

## Correlation Analysis

The correlation heatmap shows relationships between numerical variables such as Sales, Profit, Discount, and Quantity.

## Business Insights

Visualization helps identify profitable categories and suitable discount levels. These insights can support better pricing and sales decisions.

## Conclusion

Data visualization makes retail data easier to understand and helps businesses improve profitability and decision-making.
